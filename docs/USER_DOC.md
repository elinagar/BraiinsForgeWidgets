# User Documentation

## Party Quiz

Party Quiz turns the Deck into a trivia game show. The Deck is the board; every player's phone is a buzzer.

### Setting up

1. In the Deck's web app, add the **Party Quiz** widget to a fullscreen scene. It needs the whole 1280x480 panel.
2. Optional settings: **Category pack** (Mixed, Science and space, Geography, History, Film, TV and music, Sport, Food
   and drink, Animals and nature, Technology, Bitcoin and mining, Kids), **Difficulty** (Easy, Medium, Hard, Mixed),
   **Questions per game** (5 to 30), **Answer time** (10 to 40 seconds), **Show player names on Deck**, **Sounds**.
3. Turn off scene cycling for the evening, or disable the other scenes. Phones can only talk to the widget while its
   scene is on screen; if the Deck cycles away, phones see "The Deck is showing another scene" until it comes back.
4. Swipe the Deck to that scene. The lobby shows a QR code and the address underneath it.

### Joining

- Everyone connects their phone to the same Wi-Fi as the Deck and scans the QR code, or types the address into the
  browser. There is no app and no account.
- Type a name (up to 12 characters) and pick a colour. Colours already taken are greyed out while free ones remain;
  after six players they repeat.
- Up to 12 players fit. The lobby shows who is in, and the Deck's lights flash in each newcomer's colour.
- The first player to join is the host and gets the Start, Skip, and End controls on their phone. If the host leaves,
  the next player in join order takes over.

### Playing

- The Deck shows the question and four answers, each in a fixed colour and shape: red triangle, blue diamond, amber
  circle, green square. Your phone shows the same four shapes without the text, so everyone looks at the board.
- Tap the shape of your answer. The first tap locks it and the Deck gives a quiet tap. The ring on the Deck counts
  down; the lights turn amber for the last five seconds and tick for the last three.
- A correct answer scores 1 000 points in the first instant, sliding down to 500 at the deadline. Three correct answers
  in a row add a 200-point bonus to every further correct answer until you miss.
- The reveal highlights the right card, shows how the room voted and who was fastest, and adds a one-line
  explanation. The lights breathe green when at least half the room was right, red otherwise. Your phone tells you
  whether you got it, your points, your rank, and your streak.
- The standings show after every question. After the last one the podium appears with the winner's colour on the
  lights. The host can tap **New game** to return to the lobby with the same players, or the Deck returns on its own
  after 30 seconds.

### Good to know

- Left the page or locked your phone? Open the address again and you are back in your seat with your score.
- The Deck remembers its best-ever scores and shows the record in the lobby.
- The built-in pack has 460 questions, 46 per category. Questions are not repeated until the whole pack for the chosen
  settings has been played.
- Stuck players or a broken round? The host's phone has **Restart** in every view; it kicks everyone, and all rejoin by
  scanning again. The **Reset** button in the Deck's top bar does the same from the device, in any phase.
- The game runs entirely on your Wi-Fi. Nothing about players or answers leaves the Deck.
- On a guest network with client isolation, phones cannot reach the Deck. Join the same network the Deck uses.
